## Why This Exists

Most clipboard workflows force you to copy one item at a time, lose previous clips immediately, and jump between scattered notes or scratch files just to retrieve something you copied minutes ago. **Maccy** solves this fragmentation by putting clipboard history, search, and retrieval into one lightweight environment.

Maccy solves **one specific problem**: lost copy history on macOS. Just open Maccy, start typing, and see your entire clipboard history with instant search and keyboard-first navigation. The Maccy clipboard manager approach keeps every copied item within reach without interrupting your focus.

If you are tired of losing that snippet, URL, or code fragment you copied three steps ago, **Maccy** is for you.
